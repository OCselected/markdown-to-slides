## Slide 31: 

* 视觉隐喻：
  * 亿万年的地球变迁
* 显示要点：
  * Throughout the 150 years, innovations such as the internal combustion engine, airplanes, vacuum tubes, antibiotics, transistors, semiconductors, personal computers, and the internet profoundly changed living standards. 
  * Importantly, many of these are what economic historians call “general purpose technologies” (GPTs) whose transformative effects extend throughout the economy. 
  * For A.I., the clear lesson from economic history is that the effects on GDP and productivity may take longer to appear than we expect.
  *  AI remarkably stable economic growth at 2% per year （ just as they were with electricity and the internet）

## Slide 4 ：Weak link

* 视觉隐喻：

* 显示要点：
  * Task-based models of economic growth provide key insights to help us understand the effects of automation
  * the replacement of labor in weaving textiles by mechanical looms.
  * cars and trucks replace people and horses in transportation, 
  * tractors replace people in many agricultural tasks, 
  * electronic computers replace human computers in performing calculations.
  * Nevertheless, the explosion is remarkably gradual.

  * in principle the large increases in GDP could make everyone better off: it becomes a question of distribution. 
  *  jobs provide a foundation for meaning.
