## Slide 27
- (Tim o‘reilly view)open source community is not beholden to a timeframe to deliver a useful and capable product. Anyone who has lived in the high-technology industry for any length of time will be well-aware of situations where products are released before their time. When these situations occur, the product is often shelved and never again sees the light of day. The free software community doesn't have such restrictions and technology can find new uses when the community is finally ready for it.
- ———— Martin Fink《The Business and Economics of Linux and Open Source》
