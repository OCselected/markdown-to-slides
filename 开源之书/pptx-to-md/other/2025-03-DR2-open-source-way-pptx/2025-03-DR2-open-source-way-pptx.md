## Slide 5

Platforms, ecosystems, and open source projects proliferated at the end of the twentieth century in response to the opportunities inherent in new, fast-changing digital technologies. None of these “new forms of organizing” was entirely new. Horizontally layered industries, platforms, ecosystems, and collaborative R&D projects have existed in various settings for a long time. However, the spread of these new organizational forms in conjunction with digital technologies was both rapid and remarkable. The new organizations’ rise to prominence in the late twentieth deserves an explanation. Yet today, we lack a general theory to explain and help us understand the relationship between technologies and organizations. This book seeks to develop such a theory.
